# Privacy Policy --- Chat Bot Support AI

**Last updated:** October 20, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Chat Bot Support AI ("we," "our," or "the app") provides automated
chatbot and customer-support functionality. This Privacy Policy explains
how information may be handled when you interact with the app.

## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled, the app may process information you
choose to send, such as: - Messages and questions you submit to the
chatbot. - Your public profile name or basic account identifiers made
available by the platform. - Technical information needed to operate,
secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government
identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information through
the chatbot.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to: - Respond to your messages and support
requests. - Operate, maintain, and improve the chatbot. - Detect abuse,
troubleshoot errors, and protect the service. - Meet applicable legal
obligations.

## 4. Sharing and Third-Party Services

The app may rely on platform providers and service providers needed to
operate the chatbot, such as hosting, automation, or AI-processing
services. Information is shared only as needed to provide the service,
maintain security, or comply with law. Those providers may process
information under their own privacy terms.

## 5. Retention and Security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the
purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by
law. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information, but no
online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may stop interacting with the chatbot at any time. To request access
to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your
interaction, contact **workyassin132@gmail.com** from the email address
you wish us to use for follow-up. Include enough detail to identify the
request, but do not send passwords or sensitive identity documents. We
will review and respond to requests as required by applicable law.

## 7. Children

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from
children where parental consent is required by law.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be
made available at the page where this policy is published, with the
updated date shown above.
